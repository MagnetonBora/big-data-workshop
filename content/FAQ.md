# What is the difference between **SparkContext** and **SparkSession**?

**SparkContext** is the low-level entry point to Spark used primarily for the RDD API, while **SparkSession** is the high-level unified entry point for working with SQL, DataFrames, and Datasets, and it internally contains a SparkContext. More details [[SparkContext vs SparkSession|here]].
